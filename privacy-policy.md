# Spark™ — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-10-05_

Spark™ is an offline-first gratitude, wins & affirmations app developed by **Lamont Labs**.

## Data Collection
Spark™ **does not collect, share, or transmit** any personal data. All entries, streak data, and preferences remain stored locally on your device using SQLite.

## Network & Permissions
The app requests **no network permissions**. It cannot connect to the internet. No analytics, ads, telemetry, or trackers are used.

## Storage
All data is stored locally in an encrypted database file (`spark.db`) on your device.

## User Control
You can delete your data anytime by clearing the app’s storage or using “Reset Journal” in the Settings screen. Deleting the app permanently erases all local data.

## Children & Families
Spark™ offers kid, teen, and parent modes. The app is family-friendly and contains no external links or purchases.
