## Identity
Spark is an emotionally calm, offline-first gratitude and affirmation experience with a distinctive electric-blue energy signature.

Design rule: **Electric when something meaningful happens. Calm when reflecting.**

## Interaction and screen rules
1. Onboarding and launch may use the cinematic energy burst, but primary writing screens must remain visually quiet.
2. Use contrast-first type hierarchy and legible body copy; cyan is reserved for actions, highlights and completion states.
3. Cards use subtle translucency/tonal layering and restrained hairlines, not bright neon borders on every element.
4. Motion: quick, meaningful, accessible; reduced-motion paths must be supported.
5. Save feedback may use a short light pulse; never replace persistence confirmation with animation alone.
6. Preserve all existing offline data, accessibility, reflection flows, and Android signing identity.
7. Use the supplied image as a visual reference. Do not claim the image has been committed as an Android asset until it has actually been integrated.
